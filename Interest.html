Beyond Technology

Whether I am testing an off-grid communication setup, designing a 3D-printed part, or getting out on the water, I enjoy activities that combine problem-solving, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

Paddleboarding is a way to disconnect from screens and explore Florida's lakes, springs, and calm waterways. It combines exercise, balance, outdoor awareness, and an appreciation for the natural environment.

Meshtastic and LoRa-based mesh communication are interesting examples of low-power technology supporting decentralized, off-grid messaging. I enjoy learning about radio devices, antennas, node placement, coverage testing, and mesh-network design concepts.
